# Design B Context

Design B is Moof's calm, ingredient-led concept. Use the terms below when discussing or maintaining this variant so its identity stays distinct from the other client bake-off designs.

## Language

**Curated Ingredients**:
The overall Design B direction: a clear, calming presentation of Moof's supplied tea, milk, and seasonal drink facts. It helps visitors understand source, ritual, flavour, and choice without making health or medical claims.
_Avoid_: Wellness cure, nutritional promise, clinical language

**Tea Standards**:
The source-led section that introduces Mori and Shiran and describes the supplied role each matcha plays in a drink. It should feel precise, easy to scan, and grounded in the provided facts.
_Avoid_: Health outcome, supplier profile, tea lecture

**Preparation Ritual**:
A short explanation of how supplied matcha drinks are prepared and chosen. It is a calm brand moment, not a performance claim or comparison.
_Avoid_: Cure ritual, performance benefit, superiority claim

**Drink Choice**:
The menu-facing presentation of flavour, temperature, and milk options. It supports easy selection rather than urgency or persuasion through scarcity.
_Avoid_: Prescription, personalised health plan, limited-time promise
