# Design B Context

Design B is Moof's playful, high-energy concept. Use the terms below when discussing or maintaining this variant so its identity stays distinct from the other client bake-off designs.

## Language

**Playful Reinterpretation**:
The overall Design B direction: a loud, rounded, sticker-like Moof expression applied to a familiar tea-shop content sequence. It borrows structure, not visual assets or trade dress.
_Avoid_: Tsujiri clone, youth theme, generic neubrutalism

**Ingredient Story**:
The provenance-led section that introduces Mori and Shiran and explains how each matcha behaves in a drink. It should make ingredient knowledge quick, vivid, and conversational.
_Avoid_: About copy, supplier profile, tea lecture

**Seasonal Drop**:
A currently available rotating drink presented with urgency and playful energy. Seasonal Drops remain separate from the Core Lineup and change as a complete owner-supplied batch.
_Avoid_: Limited edition unless the owner uses that term, permanent special

**Social Mosaic**:
The colorful image grid and Instagram call to action near the end of the homepage. It is a composed brand moment, not a live social feed.
_Avoid_: Instagram feed, gallery widget
