# Design B Brand And Copy Rules

Use these rules with the shared Moof AI knowledge base when maintaining Design B.

## Preserve

- Chunky rounded display type paired with a friendly, readable body face.
- Thick ink outlines, hard offset shadows, rounded cards, and sticker-like badges.
- Saturated matcha green, yellow, orange, sky, and lilac color blocks.
- A clear phone-first hierarchy: promise, Ingredient Story, products, location, Social Mosaic.
- Energetic, conversational copy with short sentences and specific drink details.
- Separate Core Lineup and Seasonal Drop groups.

## Copy Voice

Write like an enthusiastic, informed friend: confident, playful, and concise. A small joke or contemporary phrase is welcome when it clarifies the mood of a drink. Keep prices, ingredients, availability, directions, and operating hours literal and easy to scan. Never invent provenance, customer attribution, product claims, or store facts.

## Avoid

- Luxury language, hushed editorial phrasing, or generic wellness claims.
- Dense paragraphs, unexplained tea jargon, or jokes that obscure factual information.
- Softening the design into neutral minimalism or replacing hard shadows with glossy effects.
- White text on the bright orange accent; use ink for accessible contrast.
- Copying Tsujiri assets, logos, illustrations, exact compositions, or trade dress. Tsujiri may guide the content sequence only.

## Maintenance Notes

The Social Mosaic is a curated local-image composition, not a live Instagram embed. Keep its image paths local and retain the explicit `@moof__bar` link. During the bake-off, menu facts live in `src/data/menu.js`; treat the seasonal array as one Seasonal Drop batch unless the owner explicitly requests a narrower edit.
