# Brand And Copy Rules

## Brand

- Use `Moof` exactly.
- Keep the wordmark rounded, soft, and playful.
- Use matcha green as the main product signal.
- Use warm orange, peach, and soft rose tones from the photographed menu background.
- Use white or cream display type for large hero copy.

## Voice

Moof should sound like a modern specialty cafe: direct, sensory, and a little playful. Avoid formal luxury-tea language and generic wellness claims.

## Layout

The first screen should feel product-led: oversized matcha type, a large drink image, and a horizontal drink carousel. Keep the menu and about sections close to the first screen so phone users can move through the page quickly.

The carousel should feel active: the selected drink appears large in the hero, while the other carousel cards are smaller and visually muted. Keep arrow buttons visible on the left and right of the hero.

Users should change the selected hero drink by clicking a card or arrow. Do not make horizontal rail scrolling change the hero selection.

On wide screens, the selected drink may have compact supporting panels: review on the left and tasting ratings on the right. On phones, stack these panels so the product image stays clear and nothing overlaps.

Do not put a green decorative blob behind the featured drink. Let the transparent drink asset sit directly on the warm Moof background with a subtle drop shadow.

Keep supporting copy visually lowercase. Hero display words may stay title case or uppercase when they are intentional headline treatment, but descriptions, location notes, tags, and small support text should read lowercase.

The custom announcement should feel like a compact promo band with image support. The about section should be an open two-column layout on desktop: story and ingredient copy on the left, matcha image on the right, without cards around the story. The find-us section may use compact cards with simple line icons.

## Copy Examples

Good:

- "Seasonal drinks are pouring now."
- "Exclusive matcha ingredients, layered fresh with milk, oat milk, fruit, sesame, and hojicha."
- "A richer pour using Moof's exclusive matcha profile."

Avoid:

- "Experience ancient wellness in every sip."
- "The ultimate premium ceremonial journey."
- "Revolutionizing beverages with innovative solutions."
