# Design C Context

Design C is Moof's aesthetics-first concept for an audience that discovers cafés through visual identity, seasonal drinks, and shareable moments. It keeps the same business facts as the other bake-off variants while presenting them through restrained editorial composition.

## Language

**Editorial Calm**: The warm, spacious visual system that uses quiet typography, measured pacing, and product imagery instead of loud promotion.
_Avoid_: Luxury tea, minimalist for its own sake

**Seasonal Edit**: A curated, three-drink presentation sourced directly from the current seasonal menu data. It is a visual selection, not a second menu or separate content source.
_Avoid_: Collection, product drop

**Photo Moment**: A composed product view that makes a drink easy to notice, remember, and share without inventing claims about the café or its customers.
_Avoid_: Instagram bait, viral moment

**Quiet Hospitality**: Warm, concise language that helps a guest feel welcome while respecting the calm page rhythm.
_Avoid_: Premium exclusivity, sales copy

For shared business terms such as Maintenance, Source of Truth, Seasonal Update Batch, and Preview Review, follow the repository-wide glossary and platform documentation.
