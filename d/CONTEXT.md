# Design D Context

Design D is Moof's conversion-first mobile concept. It is an independent presentation of the same store and menu facts used across the bake-off.

## Language

**Walk-In Conversion**:
The primary outcome of the page: helping a nearby phone-in-hand visitor decide to visit Moof and open directions with minimal friction. It does not mean online ordering, booking, or checkout.
_Avoid_: Funnel, transaction, lead capture

**Directions CTA**:
The repeated action that opens `site.directionsUrl`, including the persistent phone bar. It stays prominent because store visits are Design D's primary goal.
_Avoid_: Order now, book now, generic learn more

**Seasonal Urgency**:
Direct, time-sensitive presentation of the currently supplied seasonal menu. Urgency may describe limited batches or rotation, but must not invent dates, quantities, or availability.
_Avoid_: Fake scarcity, countdown campaign

**Social Proof**:
Customer quotes and source labels supplied in the site's data. Preserve the supplied wording and attribution; never infer or invent a customer's identity.
_Avoid_: Testimonials without a source label, fabricated attribution
