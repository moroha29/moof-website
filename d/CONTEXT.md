# Design D Context

Design D is Moof's deliberately simplistic concept. It is an independent presentation of the same store and menu facts used across the bake-off.

## Language

**Simplistic**:
The overall Design D direction: a pared-back presentation with generous whitespace, a small palette, decisive type, and only the information needed to choose a drink or find the store.
_Avoid_: Campaign landing page, decorative overload, conversion funnel

**Quiet Navigation**:
A minimal, accessible Home/Menu navigation treatment. It is clear but intentionally unobtrusive, with no persistent or sticky conversion bar.
_Avoid_: Sticky CTA, order now, book now

**Featured Drinks**:
A short, image-led selection from the supplied menu that gives the homepage focus without implying popularity, urgency, or limited availability.
_Avoid_: Bestseller, staff pick, limited drop

**Simple Menu**:
A spacious, factual product list with name, price, description, and supplied options. It is for scanning, not persuasion through reviews or elaborate category chrome.
_Avoid_: Testimonials, countdowns, dense comparison tables
